About Us

A Family Story
Spanning Decades

What began as one man's craft has grown into a legacy of excellence. Our commitment to attention to detail, durability, and customer satisfaction has been passed down through the years, making us a trusted name throughout Liverpool and beyond.

1962
Joe Woodcock established the foundations of the company through traditional craftsmanship and skilled workmanship, building a reputation that would endure for generations.
1979
Colin took over the business and continued to grow its reputation for reliability, attention to detail, and high-quality work across Liverpool.
1983
Alan joined the company. Together, Colin and Alan spent decades delivering stained glass, precision glazing, and professional installation services for homes and businesses alike.
2026
Colin and Alan retired together, proudly passing the company on to Stephen Woodcock and the next generation — honouring its heritage while bringing fresh ideas and modern expertise.
Our Process

How We Create
Stained Glass

Every stained glass commission follows an eight-stage process refined over 42 years — from the first conversation to the final installation. Here's how we bring your vision to life.

01
Consultation & Design
Every project begins with a personal consultation. We work closely with you to understand your style, space, and vision before creating a bespoke design tailored to your property.
02
Choosing the Glass
We carefully select high-quality coloured, textured, or decorative glass to suit the design. We help you choose the right combination of colours and finishes for the perfect result.
03
Hand Cutting
Each individual piece of glass is precisely hand-cut by our skilled craftsmen using traditional techniques passed down through three generations of joiners and glass fitters.
04
Detailed Craftsmanship
Where required, decorative details and patterns are added to the glass to create unique and personalised designs. Every piece is crafted with attention to detail and accuracy.
05
Assembly & Leading
The glass pieces are assembled using traditional leadwork methods, carefully fitted together to form a strong and beautifully balanced stained glass panel.
06
Strengthening & Finishing
Our team solders and reinforces every joint to ensure long-lasting durability. Each panel is thoroughly cleaned and inspected before installation.
07
Professional Installation
We professionally install the stained glass into windows, doors, or custom joinery features, ensuring a secure fit and flawless appearance.
08
A Tradition of Quality
With 42 years of experience in stained glass, we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques to create timeless designs that add character, elegance, and individuality.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked
Questions

How does the process start?

We begin with a consultation to understand your needs, style preferences, and project requirements. From there, we provide expert advice and a detailed quotation.

Do you offer bespoke joinery and glass solutions?

Yes, all of our work can be fully customised to suit your space and design vision, whether it's fitted furniture, glazing, partitions, or specialist installations.

How long does a project usually take?

Project timelines vary depending on the size and complexity of the work. After the initial consultation, we will provide a clear timeframe and keep you updated throughout the process.

Do you handle both residential and commercial projects?

Absolutely. We work with homeowners, businesses, contractors, and developers on projects of all sizes.

What materials do you use?

We use high-quality timber, glass, and fittings to ensure durability, safety, and a premium finish on every project.

Will I receive a quotation before work begins?

Yes, we provide transparent, no-obligation quotations before any work starts, so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes, our experienced team handles both manufacturing and professional installation to ensure every detail is completed to the highest standard.

Why choose our company?

With three generations of experience, we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques, delivering reliable service, quality workmanship, and attention to detail on every project.
