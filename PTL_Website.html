The problem

Most manufacturers treat connections
as a procurement decision.

Product contaminationA single connection failure in a sterile line can trigger a batch loss worth tens of thousands. The gasket that caused it may have cost less than £5.
02
Compliance exposureA connection finding in an FDA or MHRA inspection can trigger facility-wide remediation programmes.
